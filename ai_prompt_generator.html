How to Use This Page

You can use this tool in two ways depending on how much detail you already have ready.

This section uses a longer video prompt because the final video should be professional, natural and detailed enough for a portfolio, interview preparation or job application.

      

        
3-5 Minute Professional Introduction Video Script

        

          
Minimum length: 3-5 minutes.

Purpose: help employers understand who you are, what you can do, what evidence you have, and what career direction you are pursuing.

          
Act as a professional video scriptwriter, career coach and communication trainer.

Create a natural, confident and professional 3-5 minute introduction video script for my digital portfolio.

My details:
Name:
Current role or study area:
Target career field:
Target job roles:
Qualifications:
Certifications:
Relevant experience:
Technical skills:
Transferable skills:
Best projects:
Achievements:
Career goal:
Personal strengths:
Why I am interested in this field:
What makes me different:
LinkedIn/GitHub/website links to mention:
Preferred tone: confident, professional, warm, natural and not robotic

The video must be between 3 and 5 minutes when spoken clearly.

Please structure the script as follows:
1. Opening greeting - 15 to 20 seconds
2. Professional background - 35 to 45 seconds
3. Skills and strengths - 45 to 60 seconds
4. Project evidence - 60 to 90 seconds
5. Professional values and working style - 30 to 45 seconds
6. Career goal and value to employers - 30 to 45 seconds
7. Closing statement - 20 to 30 seconds

Also include a 90-second version, rehearsal bullets, camera delivery tips, pacing tips, mistakes to avoid, background and lighting advice, and a final recording checklist.

VARCS Evidence Check

      
VARCS helps you check whether your portfolio evidence is Valid, Authentic, Reliable, Current and Sufficient.

      

        
VARCS Portfolio Evidence Review

        

          
Use this after building your CV, LinkedIn, GitHub and project evidence.

          
Act as a portfolio assessor and evidence quality reviewer.

Review my digital portfolio using the VARCS framework.

My target role:
My CV:
My LinkedIn profile summary:
My GitHub/projects:
My website content:
My certificates:
My project evidence:

Assess my evidence against:
V - Valid: Does the evidence directly relate to my target role?
A - Authentic: Does the evidence look genuinely mine and clearly show my contribution?
R - Reliable: Is the evidence consistent, well-documented and credible?
C - Current: Is the evidence recent and relevant to current job-market expectations?
S - Sufficient: Is there enough evidence to convince an employer?

Please provide:
1. A VARCS score for each area
2. Strengths
3. Weak evidence
4. Missing evidence
5. Recommended improvements
6. Priority action list
7. Final employer-readiness judgement

Best used for the final review before applying.

          
Act as a recruitment consultant and application reviewer.

Review my application using the JOBS framework.

Job description:
My CV:
My cover letter:
My LinkedIn profile:
My project evidence:
My GitHub/website links:

Use this framework:
J - Job Match: How well does my application match the specific role and company?
O - Optimise: Have I used the right keywords, language and structure?
B - Back It Up: Does every important claim have evidence, project proof, certificate or result?
S - Submit Professionally: Are my files, formatting, tone, links and final presentation professional?

Please provide:
1. Score out of 100

3. Weaknesses

5. Claims that need evidence
6. Suggested CV edits
